# verlet-rope

rope physics for roblox with verlet integration, no constraints or physics parts.

<img src="progress/04-bridge.gif" width="720" alt="crates piling on a bridge until it snaps">

`rojo serve` or paste src/Rope.luau into a ModuleScript in ReplicatedStorage and src/Demo.client.luau into a LocalScript in StarterPlayerScripts, then hit play. drag the rope with the mouse, pull hard to snap it, B drops a crate on the bridge, R resets everything.

wip, see [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md) for how it got here
